RESPECT & STYLE

How to notice someone's style respectfully

A photo can be appreciated for its color, clothing, lighting, and composition. The person in it still deserves privacy and respect.

Advertisement

Look at the choices, not assumptions

Notice the styling: the cut and color of the clothes, how the outfit works together, the setting, or the photographer's use of light. Those are visible creative choices. A pose or outfit does not tell you someone's personality, availability, or interest.

Keep comments considerate

If you want to compliment someone in person, focus on something they chose, such as a jacket or color combination. Keep it brief, give them space to respond, and accept a short answer or no response without pressing. Avoid intimate comments about a stranger's body.

Respect the person behind the image

Clothing and body language are not consent. Do not touch, photograph, share, or identify someone without permission. When viewing a published image, appreciate the work while remembering the subject is a person with boundaries.

The gallery photos are editorial images. They are not evidence of a person's private life, health, measurements, or consent.
