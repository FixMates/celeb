COMFORT & FIT

How to choose a bra size

A size on a label is only a starting point. The best fit is comfortable, supportive, and suited to the style you're trying.

Advertisement

Measure, then use the brand's chart

  1. Measure around your rib cage just under the bust, keeping the tape level and comfortably snug.
  2. Measure around the fullest part of the bust without pulling the tape tight.
  3. Use the specific brand's size chart to find a starting size. Sizing and shape vary between brands and bra styles, so measurements cannot guarantee a fit.

Check the fit while wearing it

  • Band: It should feel firm and level around the body without pinching or riding up.
  • Cups: Breast tissue should sit comfortably inside, without spilling over or leaving large gaps. The wire, if present, should rest around the breast rather than on it.
  • Centre: On many underwire styles, the centre piece should sit close to the chest. Some designs naturally fit differently.
  • Straps: Adjust them so they stay put without digging in. They should not have to carry all the support.

Try a different cup or band, or another cut, if the bra pinches, shifts, gaps, or feels uncomfortable. Recheck sizing when your body or preferred style changes. A professional fitter can help if you want one.

The gallery images are for editorial context only; they are not bra-fitting demonstrations. Fit should be judged by the wearer's own comfort.
