EDITORIAL NOTES

How to read these figures

Birth dates and measurements are taken from the linked public profile pages. Those pages are community-submitted, and measurements can be incomplete, inconsistent, or out of date. Age is calculated from the listed birth date. “Bust” displays the reported bra size when one is available; waist and hip values are shown in inches. Hip measurement is a circumference figure, not a separate “butt size.”

Empty image slots use original generic fashion diagrams, fully clothed and not to scale. Profile images are supplied by the site owner and are not represented as official or current photographs.

TerraBox links and image paths are stored with each performer in profiles.json.
